Privacy Policy

Raiden Dojo Postiz · Effective 29 October 2026

Raiden Dojo Postiz is a personal publishing tool run by Raiden Dojo. It schedules short video clips that the owner has reviewed and approved, and posts them to the owner's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and X through each platform's official API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app receives

When the owner connects one of their own accounts, the platform gives the app:

  • an access token (and, where the platform provides one, a refresh token) that lets the app publish on that account;
  • basic profile information for that account, such as its ID, display name, username and profile picture.

The app does not request or collect information about other people, such as followers, viewers or commenters, beyond what a platform shows alongside the owner's own posts.

How it is used

This information is used only to publish the owner's posts and to show whether each post succeeded. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and it is never sold, rented or shared with anyone.

Where it is stored

Tokens and profile information are stored on the owner's own computer, in the app's local database. They are not uploaded to any other server, except when sent to the platform they belong to as part of an API request.

Retention and deletion

Information for an account is kept while that account is connected. Disconnecting the account in the app deletes its tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time from the platform itself, for example TikTok's Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
